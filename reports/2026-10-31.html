오늘의 짧은 영어 아티클

Halloween falls on October 31 every year. In the United States, children wear costumes and go from house to house to collect candy. They say "trick or treat!" at each door. In Korea, Halloween is not a traditional holiday, but it has become popular in recent years, especially among young people. Many cafes and stores in big cities decorate with pumpkins and spooky decorations. Some people also remember a sad crowd accident in Seoul a few years ago, so safety has become an important topic during Halloween events. Whether you celebrate it or not, October 31 is a good day to think about autumn, costumes, and community safety.

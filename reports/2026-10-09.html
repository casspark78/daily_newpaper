SECTION 13 · ENGLISH FOR TODAY

오늘의 짧은 영어 아티클

Today is Hangeul Day in South Korea. It happens every year on October 9th. The holiday celebrates the Korean alphabet, called Hangeul. A king named Sejong created this alphabet a long time ago, in 1443. Before Hangeul, most Korean people could not read or write easily. The old writing system used Chinese characters, and it was very hard to learn. King Sejong wanted every person, not just scholars, to be able to read. So he made a simple alphabet with clear letters and shapes. He announced it to the public in 1446. This year, 2026, marks 580 years since that announcement. Today, many Koreans visit museums or join small events to remember this history.

This week, big news arrived from Stockholm. The Nobel Prize in Literature for 2026 went to Anne Carson, a poet and classics scholar from Canada. She writes poems and essays that mix ancient Greek myths with modern life. Her most famous book, "Autobiography of Red," retells an old Greek myth from a monster's point of view. After the prize was announced, people rushed to buy her books. In South Korea, sales of her novel jumped more than 300 times in just one day. Many readers who had never heard of her are now curious about her strange and poetic style.

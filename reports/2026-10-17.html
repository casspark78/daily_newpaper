This week brought a dramatic rollercoaster to South Korea's stock market. On Thursday, the Kospi index plunged more than seven percent, and trading was paused for twenty minutes because of a rare circuit breaker. Investors were worried about the cost of artificial intelligence investments and rising U.S. interest rates. The very next day, the market bounced back strongly, as many investors felt the sharp drop had been overdone. Meanwhile, oil prices spiked around the world due to rising tension in the Middle East, pushing Brent crude close to 110 dollars a barrel. Baseball fans also had something tense to watch this weekend, as the LG Twins and the KIA Tigers began their playoff series in Gwangju. It was a week full of ups and downs, both in the market and on the field.

오늘의 짧은 영어 아티클

Today is Cheoseo, a solar term that marks the end of summer heat in the Korean lunar calendar. On paper, the worst of summer should be behind us by now. But this year, many parts of Korea are still seeing daytime temperatures above 30 degrees Celsius. Farmers and weather forecasters say the gap between the calendar and the actual weather has grown wider in recent years. Some people joke that Cheoseo now feels more like a promise than a fact. Still, mornings and evenings are noticeably cooler than a month ago, so the season is slowly turning after all.

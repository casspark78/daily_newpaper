SECTION 13 · ENGLISH FOR TODAY

오늘의 짧은 영어 아티클

Jun works at a small company in Seoul. On Friday afternoon, his coworkers started talking about the weekend. Jun said he wanted to watch the baseball playoff game that night. His friend laughed and said the weather was getting cold, so Jun should wear a warm jacket at the stadium. Jun checked the forecast and saw that tomorrow would be sunny but chilly, with a big difference between morning and afternoon temperatures. He decided to bring a thermos of hot tea to the game. Before leaving the office, he wished his coworkers a good weekend.

  • playoff (명사) — 플레이오프
  • stadium (명사) — 경기장
  • thermos (명사) — 보온병

Q. What did Jun decide to bring to the baseball game?

SECTION 14 · ENGLISH PATTERNS

오늘의 영어 패턴

  • I couldn't have [무엇을 했] without you
    당신 없었으면 못 했을 거예요
    Ex. I couldn't have won this series without my teammates.
    팀원들이 없었다면 이 시리즈에서 이기지 못했을 거예요.
    실전 상황별 스피킹
  • I am [정도] worried about [무엇]
    [그것]이 [어느 정도] 걱정돼요
    Ex. I am a little worried about the stock market today.
    오늘 주식 시장이 조금 걱정돼요.
    실전 상황별 스피킹
  • when it comes to [주제], [대상] might be the best option
    [그 얘기라면] [이것]이 최선일 수 있어요
    Ex. When it comes to a quiet weekend walk, Pocheon Art Valley might be the best option.
    조용한 주말 산책이라면, 포천 아트밸리가 최선일 수 있어요.
    실전 상황별 스피킹

SECTION 11 · ENGLISH FOR TODAY

오늘의 짧은 영어 아티클

In many Korean apartment complexes, an order placed late at night is sitting on the doorstep before sunrise. This service, known as "dawn delivery," began less than a decade ago with fresh groceries and quickly expanded to nearly everything a household might need. The idea was simple but demanding: promise that anything ordered before midnight would arrive by seven the next morning, no matter how far the warehouse. Building the cold-chain logistics and overnight delivery networks to make that promise reliable took massive investment, and competition among companies pushed delivery windows even earlier. Today, dawn delivery is so common in large cities that many people plan their morning routine around it, treating a doorstep box of milk and vegetables as an ordinary part of waking up rather than a small logistical miracle.

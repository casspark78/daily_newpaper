오늘의 짧은 영어 아티클

The 20th Asian Games will begin soon in Japan. The host cities are Aichi and Nagoya, and the games run from September 19 to October 4. Korea is sending about 1,050 people, including athletes and staff members. They will compete in 41 different sports, from swimming to taekwondo. Many Korean fans are already excited to cheer for their favorite teams. This week, several athletes are doing their final training before they fly to Japan. It will be an exciting few weeks for sports fans across Asia.

SECTION 14 · ENGLISH PATTERNS

오늘의 영어 패턴

  • I went to [장소]
    나 [어디] 갔었어
    Ex. I went to Changwon to watch the LG Twins game yesterday.
    어제 나 창원에 LG 트윈스 경기 보러 갔었어.
    초급 1
  • I try to [일상적인 일을 하다] by [하루 중 시간]
    나는 [몇 시]까지 ~하려고 해
    Ex. I try to check the market news by 8 AM every morning.
    나는 매일 아침 8시까지 시장 뉴스를 확인하려고 해.
    초급 3
  • I always [일과를 하다] [하루 중 시간]
    나는 [언제] 항상 ~해
    Ex. I always take a walk in the evening when the weather is nice.
    나는 날씨가 좋으면 저녁에 항상 산책해.
    초급 3

SECTION 13 · ENGLISH FOR TODAY

오늘의 짧은 영어 아티클

Jamsil Baseball Stadium in Seoul is playing its final season in 2026. Tonight, the LG Twins and the Doosan Bears will meet there for their last rivalry game ever at this old ballpark. Jamsil Stadium opened in 1982, so it has a history of more than 40 years. After this season ends, the city of Seoul plans to tear the stadium down and build a new domed stadium in its place. The new stadium will have about 35,000 seats and is expected to open around 2031. During the construction years, both LG and Doosan will use the Olympic Stadium instead. Many baseball fans feel sad about losing such an old and meaningful ballpark.

SECTION 09 · ENGLISH FOR TODAY

오늘의 짧은 영어 아티클

When Korea's stock market swings hard in a single direction, you may notice trading briefly freeze under something called a "sidecar." A sidecar is a short, automatic pause in program trading, triggered when futures prices move sharply up or down. Unlike a circuit breaker, which halts the entire market, a sidecar only pauses computer-driven program trades for five minutes, giving human traders a brief window to catch up before prices move too fast. Korea's KOSPI and KOSDAQ both triggered buy-side sidecars this week, a rare event that usually signals unusually strong, fast-moving optimism rather than panic. Together with circuit breakers, sidecars are part of a layered system designed to keep markets orderly even when sentiment swings from fear to excitement within the same week.

  • sidecar (명사) — 사이드카, 프로그램 매매 일시 정지 제도
  • program trading (명사구) — 프로그램 매매
  • sentiment (명사) — (시장) 심리, 정서

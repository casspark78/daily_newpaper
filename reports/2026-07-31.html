SECTION 09 · ENGLISH FOR TODAY

오늘의 짧은 영어 아티클

This week, Korean investors have heard one phrase again and again on the news: circuit breaker. A circuit breaker is an automatic pause in stock trading, triggered when an index falls sharply in a short period of time. The idea borrows its name from an electrical safety device that cuts power before a circuit overheats. In the stock market, the goal is similar: give panicked investors a short cooling-off period instead of letting fear spiral into an uncontrolled crash. Korea's KOSPI has triggered the mechanism several times recently, something that historically happened only a handful of times a decade. While a circuit breaker cannot fix the underlying reasons behind a market drop, it buys time, and time is often exactly what a nervous market needs.

  • circuit breaker (명사구) — 서킷브레이커, 거래 일시 중단 제도
  • trigger (동사) — 촉발시키다, 작동시키다
  • cooling-off period (명사구) — 냉각 기간, 진정 시간

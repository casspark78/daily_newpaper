SECTION 09 · ENGLISH FOR TODAY

오늘의 짧은 영어 아티클

Long before refrigerators existed, people still enjoyed cold drinks in summer. In winter, workers cut thick blocks of ice from frozen lakes and dragged them to buildings called ice houses. These were dug partly underground and packed with straw or sawdust, which slowed the melting down to almost nothing. A well-built ice house could keep its ice until the following autumn. Wealthy families served chilled desserts in August and quietly enjoyed the surprise on their guests' faces. The idea seems clever now, but it was simply ordinary technology at the time. Every generation forgets how much effort used to go into things we press a button for today.

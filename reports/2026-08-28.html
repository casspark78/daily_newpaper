오늘의 짧은 영어 아티클

South Korea's professional baseball league, the KBO, is having a record-breaking season. More than ten million fans have already attended games this year. The LG Twins lead all ten teams in home attendance, with over 1.3 million fans visiting their stadium so far. Samsung Lions and Doosan Bears follow close behind.

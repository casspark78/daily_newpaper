SECTION 13 · ENGLISH FOR TODAY

오늘의 짧은 영어 아티클

Across Korea, late October is the peak season for autumn leaves. In the mountains, maple and ginkgo trees turn bright red and yellow. Many cities also plant ginkgo trees along their streets, so even people in busy downtown areas can enjoy the colors. Weather experts say leaves usually change color first on the tallest peaks and then move down to lower areas over a few weeks. This year, cooler mornings are expected to make the colors more vivid.

SECTION 13 · ENGLISH FOR TODAY

오늘의 짧은 영어 아티클

Today is a special day on the Korean calendar. It is called "ipdong," which means the beginning of winter. From today, the weather will turn colder across the country. In Paju, the morning air already feels sharp and cold. Many Korean families use this day to prepare kimchi for the winter, a tradition called "kimjang." People also eat warm foods to stay healthy as the cold season begins. Even though winter is starting, there is still time to enjoy the last of autumn's colors this weekend.

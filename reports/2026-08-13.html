오늘의 짧은 영어 아티클

Summer heat waves are becoming more common every year. During a heat wave, temperatures stay very high for several days in a row. This kind of weather can be dangerous, especially for older people and small children. Doctors say the best way to stay safe is to drink plenty of water throughout the day, even before you feel thirsty. It also helps to stay in the shade or in air-conditioned rooms during the hottest hours of the afternoon. Wearing light, loose clothing can keep your body cooler too. Simple habits like these make a big difference when the heat index climbs.

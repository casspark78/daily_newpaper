The LG Twins are one of the most popular baseball teams in Korea. This week, they won five games in a row. On September 4, they played against the Samsung Lions in Seoul. LG was losing by three runs early in the game, but the players did not give up. They scored four runs and won the game. The closing pitcher, Ko Woo-suk, made the win even more special. He had not saved a game in the Korean league for 1,081 days because he was trying to play in the United States. When he returned to LG, he pitched the final inning and protected the team's lead. Fans were very excited to see him back on the mound.

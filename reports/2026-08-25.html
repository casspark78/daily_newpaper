Ferrari recently unveiled its first fully electric supercar, called the Luce, and it made its Korean debut this week. Instead of the usual dials, the driver's dashboard now uses an OLED display developed together with Samsung. Engineers even stacked two curved panels to create a three-dimensional look on the instrument cluster. The car's battery cells come from a South Korean battery maker as well. This shows how deeply Korean technology has become woven into the world's most famous sports car brands. Even a car that costs nearly a million dollars depends on parts made far from Italy.

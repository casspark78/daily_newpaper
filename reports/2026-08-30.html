SECTION 13 · ENGLISH FOR TODAY

오늘의 짧은 영어 아티클

Late August in Korea can still feel like summer, but small signs of autumn are starting to appear. The morning air feels a little cooler, and street vendors begin selling roasted chestnuts near subway stations. Many people also start planning trips to see the first autumn flowers, like cosmos, in nearby parks. Families begin to think about Chuseok, the big autumn harvest holiday, and start checking train tickets early.

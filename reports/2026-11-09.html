SECTION 13 · ENGLISH FOR TODAY

오늘의 짧은 영어 아티클

On November 9, 1989, something amazing happened in Berlin. For almost thirty years, a wall had divided the city into two parts: East and West. That night, thousands of people climbed onto the wall and began to break it down with their hands and simple tools. Families who had been separated for years finally met again. The fall of the Berlin Wall became a symbol of freedom for people all over the world. Today, small pieces of the wall are kept in museums as a reminder of that historic night. Every November, people in Berlin remember this special anniversary.

  • divide (동사) — 나누다, 분리시키다
  • separate (형용사) — 떨어진, 분리된
  • reminder (명사) — 상기시켜주는 것

SECTION 14 · ENGLISH PATTERNS

  • I must have been [상태] to [무엇을 하다]
    ~했다니 내가 [어땠던] 게 분명해

    주말 내내 한국시리즈 소식을 놓쳤다니 내가 정신이 딴 데 가 있었던 게 분명해.
    패턴으로 말하기
  • I could have [무엇을 했을] my [물건]
    [내 것]을 ~할 수도 있었는데
    Ex. I could have worn my warm coat this morning.
    오늘 아침 내 따뜻한 코트를 입을 수도 있었는데.
    패턴으로 말하기
  • I could have [무엇을 했을]
    ~할 수도 있었는데

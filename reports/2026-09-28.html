SECTION 13 · ENGLISH FOR TODAY

오늘의 짧은 영어 아티클

On September 28, 1928, a British scientist named Alexander Fleming made an important discovery. He was working in his lab in London and studying bacteria. Before a short trip, he left some petri dishes on his desk. When he came back, he saw something strange. A patch of mold was growing on one dish, and the bacteria near it had died. Fleming realized the mold was producing something that killed bacteria. He named this substance penicillin. Today, penicillin and other antibiotics save millions of lives every year, all because of one lucky accident.

  • mold (noun) — 곰팡이
  • discovery (noun) — 발견
  • substance (noun) — 물질

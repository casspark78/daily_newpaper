Tomorrow is September 1st, and for many Koreans, it feels like a fresh start. Even though the weather is still warm and rainy, people begin to think about new habits for the coming months. Some office workers set small goals, like waking up earlier or reading one book a month. Others simply use the new month to clean their desks and plan their schedules. This idea of a "fresh start" can also help on days when the news feels uncertain, like stock markets going up and down. Instead of worrying about every small change, it can be more helpful to focus on steady, daily habits. A calm morning routine, even on a rainy Monday, can make the rest of the day feel more manageable.

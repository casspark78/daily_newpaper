SECTION 13 · ENGLISH FOR TODAY

오늘의 짧은 영어 아티클

Nvidia is a company that makes computer chips for artificial intelligence. This week, Nvidia announced a big plan: it will buy Hugging Face, a popular platform for open-source AI models. Many researchers and small companies use Hugging Face to share and download AI tools for free. The deal is worth about 12.9 billion dollars.

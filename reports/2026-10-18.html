오늘의 짧은 영어 아티클

This Sunday in Korea was rainy and a little tense at the same time. Heavy rain moved across the country, and the northern part of Gyeonggi Province, including Paju, saw rain from early morning. Baseball fans, meanwhile, were focused on an important playoff game between the LG Twins and the KIA Tigers in Gwangju. At the same time, investors felt nervous because AI-related stocks had dropped sharply in the US market the night before. Many people worried that some AI companies had borrowed too much money to build data centers.

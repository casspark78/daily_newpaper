Around 1,500 visitors are expected to attend the forum at COEX. Global companies such as Nvidia, Cisco, and AMD are joining the event this year. The main topic is how to design better infrastructure for the future of AI. Engineers will talk about servers, cooling systems, and how to save energy in massive data centers. As AI grows more powerful, these data centers need smarter ways to stay cool and efficient. Events like this help Korea stay connected to the latest trends in AI technology.

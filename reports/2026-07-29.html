SECTION 11 · ENGLISH FOR TODAY

오늘의 짧은 영어 아티클

Every summer, as temperatures climb past thirty degrees, a small paper cup filled with shaved ice and syrup becomes one of the most common sights outside Korean convenience stores. Known as "eol음컵," or simply an ice cup, it began decades ago as a cheap way to cool down on a tight budget. Customers would buy the cup of ice for a low price, then pour their own soda or juice over it, turning a simple drink into an instant slushy. Even today, in the age of ready-made iced coffees and bottled drinks, the ice cup remains popular precisely because it lets people customize their own refreshment. It is a small, unglamorous invention, yet it captures something very Korean about turning a hot, sticky day into a moment of simple comfort.

  • shaved ice (명사구) — 빙수, 잘게 간 얼음
  • customize (동사) — 자신의 취향대로 맞추다, 커스터마이즈하다
  • unglamorous (형용사) — 화려하지 않은, 소박한

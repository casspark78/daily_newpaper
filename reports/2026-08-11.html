SECTION 12 · ENGLISH FOR TODAY

오늘의 짧은 영어 아티클

In South Korea, professional baseball sometimes takes a short pause during the hottest days of summer. This is often called a heat wave break. During the break, players can rest and recover from the extreme heat and humidity. Doctors say that playing hard outdoors in very high temperatures can be dangerous for athletes' health. After a few days off, teams return to the field with more energy and focus. Fans are always excited to watch their favorite team's first game back.

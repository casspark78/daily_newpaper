Every September, technology companies show off their newest gadgets at IFA, a large trade show in Berlin. This year, many companies at IFA 2026 focused on home robots. One robot could fold laundry all by itself. Another robot could clean the walls of a swimming pool. A Chinese company even showed a humanoid robot that could make a cup of coffee. Visitors said the robots looked less like science fiction and more like everyday helpers. Companies believe people will want robots that do small chores at home, not just robots that entertain them. Prices for these robots are still very high, so it may take a few more years before many families can actually own one.

오늘의 짧은 영어 아티클

When winter arrives in Korea, small street carts start selling a warm snack called hotteok. It is a sweet pancake filled with brown sugar, cinnamon, and sometimes nuts or seeds. The cook presses the dough flat on a hot griddle until the sugar inside melts into a sticky syrup. People often wait in line outside, holding their cold hands near the cart for warmth. Another popular treat is bungeoppang, a fish-shaped pastry filled with sweet red bean paste. Both snacks cost only a small amount of money, so students and workers buy them easily on their way home. For many Koreans, the smell of these street foods is one of the clearest signs that cold weather has truly begun.

This week, many people in Korea are getting ready for Chuseok, the Korean harvest festival. Chuseok usually falls in September or October, and families travel to see their parents and grandparents. This year, the holiday lasts from September 24th to 27th, so many roads and trains will be very busy. People often eat songpyeon, a small rice cake shaped like a half-moon, during Chuseok. Some families also visit their hometowns to enjoy the golden rice fields and cooler autumn weather. Even the stock market takes a break — it will be closed for four days during the holiday. Whether people travel far or just relax at home, Chuseok is a time to be thankful and spend time with family.

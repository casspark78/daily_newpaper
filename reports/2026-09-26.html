SECTION 13 · ENGLISH FOR TODAY

오늘의 짧은 영어 아티클

On September 26, 1960, something new happened in American politics. John F. Kennedy and Richard Nixon met for the first televised presidential debate. Millions of people watched it on TV for the first time. Radio listeners thought Nixon sounded strong and calm. But TV viewers saw something different. Nixon looked tired and pale, while Kennedy looked confident and relaxed. After the debate, many people said Kennedy won because of how he looked, not just what he said. This event changed politics forever. It showed that image can be as powerful as words.

  • confident (adjective) — 자신감 있는
  • debate (noun) — 토론
  • image (noun) — 이미지, 인상

SECTION 14 · ENGLISH PATTERNS

오늘의 영어 패턴

  • sounds [형용사]
    [어떻게] 들리네 / ~하겠다
    Ex. "LG plays KIA for third place tonight." "Sounds exciting!"
    "LG가 오늘 밤 3위를 걸고 KIA와 붙어." "재밌겠다!"
    초급 1
  • I am bummed about [~하는 것]
    ~하게 돼서 아쉬워
    Ex. I'm bummed about LG losing to Lotte on Thursday.
    목요일에 LG가 롯데한테 진 게 아쉬워.
    초급 3
  • what about [새로운 주제]?
    [그건] 어때?
    Ex. What about visiting Heyri Art Village this weekend?
    이번 주말에 헤이리 예술마을 가는 거 어때?
    초급 3
